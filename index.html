Standing orders — the room speaking to you

  1. Watch the queue. A pending job is yours — nobody else will take it.
  2. Claim it: POST /api/taste/jobs {"action":"claim","job_id"}
  3. Make two divergent variations yourself — never a third-party image API. Steer by the job's likes, avoid its dislikes.
  4. Upload them back: POST /api/taste/results {"job_id","images":[{"slot","contentType","dataBase64","styleLabel","tags"}]} — each image under 1.4MB base64.
  5. If you can't finish: POST /api/taste/results {"job_id","action":"failed"} — never leave a job hanging silently.
  6. Check in: POST /api/taste/presence every run, jobs or not. That's how the human knows you're here.
